About & privacy

Monthly Vinyl Releases is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of the shops listed. Prices, stock and release dates come from the shops' public new-release pages and are updated every morning. They may have changed since; the shop's own page is always right.

No account, no cookies, no tracking. Your bag, follows, heard records and settings are stored only in your browser. You can back them up under My bag → Your data.

Covers and audio previews load directly from the shops' servers. Some previews play via YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, only when you press play. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

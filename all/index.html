Monthly Vinyl collects new electronic vinyl releases from 24 independent record shops in Europe every morning: house, techno, electro, disco, ambient and everything in between. Listen to the previews here, compare prices, then buy the records directly from the shop. No ads, no affiliate links, no cookies.

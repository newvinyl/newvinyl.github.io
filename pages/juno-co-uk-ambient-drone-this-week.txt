 Ta3alay (feat Gumar) (2:57)
 Review: Kuwaiti producer Fatima Al Qadiri has spent a decade bending club music imagined through gender, regional and pop mythology, from 2017's Shaneera to this second Hyperdub EP since she returned to the label. She's also been busy scoring films, including Skincare, and now returns with 3TWI, Kuwaiti femme slang for dirty old man, and its four tracks are club-fit love letters to West Asian folk dance. 'Bobo Secret', 'Lama3an' and 'Gumar' riff in Kuwaiti, classical Arabic and 3eemi Farsi that is half-improvised and sounds gleefully coy. The single '7ilween' even has a Sophia Al-Maria video shot in Baghdad. Sleek and proudly queer. … Read more

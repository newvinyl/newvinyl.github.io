 BERLIN SESSIONS II
 12 Inch 23.10.26
 berlin sessions ii continues the dialogue between ray kajioka and eddie hale, two producers working across techno, electro and experimental ground. explorer opens with a rolling low end and clipped hats, built for the peak. seven, kajioka alone, strips back to a stark detroit pulse, drum machine logic laid bare. home returns the pair to shared ground, warmer pads over a steady kick, built for the room rather than the mainroom. eleven closes with hale solo, electro accents and a looser, more experimental swing. across four tracks the record moves between drive and detour, functional enough for a set, strange enough to stand apart from it.

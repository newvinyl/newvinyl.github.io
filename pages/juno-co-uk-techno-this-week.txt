 Vril - "Runaway Technology" (3:46)
 Review: Mindscape's debut 12" is ambitious in scope, with eight names across six tracks of serious techno business. Antigone's 'Off World Colonies' clocks in at barely two minutes so is a sketch rather than a statement, while Arthur Robert's near 10-minute 'Telepatthy' is a deep space odyssey. Luigi Tozzi and Antonio Ruscito split 'Memento' between them and cook up textured, metallic broken beat loops, Sama and VNTM bring a deer, headier pressure to 'Osmium' and Nadia Struiwigh weaves liquid bass and gloopy pads into a lurching bass cavern 'Plan B 17'. A strong first outing for Mindscape.

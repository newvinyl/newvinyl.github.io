 Benjamin Damage has long operated at the intersection of physical intensity and emotional depth, developing a distinct approach to Techno where machine-driven rhythms leave space for melody, atmosphere and movement. On UFO Incision, he pushes that balance across four tracks that move from expansive and melodic to increasingly stripped-back and rhythmically focused territory. Feel 4U opens with a rolling, tightly controlled groove. Dry, repetitive drums provide the drive, contrasted by bright, open pads that give the track a striking sense of space. Carefully placed vocal fragments cut through the arrangement, adding another layer of tension to a track that feels hard and propulsive while carrying a subtle melancholic depth. Solex expands the melodic side of the record. A shifting arpeggio forms its centre, moving against darker melodic tones, elevated pads and a captivating chopped vocal. Constant modulation keeps the track in motion, while a loose hi-hat groove offsets the rigidity of the low-end. Rather than settling into repetition, the arrangement continuously rises, falls and reshapes itself. The title track marks a shift in focus. Darker and more reduced, UFO Incision strips back much of the melodic language of the A- side in favour of rhythm and detail. Dry drums form a tightly locked groove, while bleeps, glitches and finely placed sonic interventions create complexity around a minimal framework. Precise and functional, it finds depth in the smallest movements. Orbitings closes the record with a warped take on acid. Swinging drums and a dry, hypnotic groove meet futuristic acid tones and deep, modulating stabs, creating a track that feels both stripped and constantly in motion. Across UFO Incision, Benjamin Damage moves between two sides of the same language: melody and function, openness and reduction, emotional weight and physical drive.

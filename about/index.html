About Monthly Vinyl

Every morning this page collects the newest electronic vinyl from independent record shops: house, techno, disco, ambient and everything in between. Listen to the previews, put what you like in your bag, and buy directly at the shop.

Not for profit. No ads, no affiliate links, no cookies.

For record shops

Your shop is not listed? We'd love to include more independent record shops, wherever they are based.

What we need: a public new-releases page or a feed that may be read automatically, with cover images and ideally audio previews. Every listing links straight to your product page. We don't sell anything ourselves.

One-tap checkout: if your shop offers a link that puts several records into the basket at once (for example /cart/add?items=123,456), buyers here can send their whole order to your checkout with a single tap.

Privacy

Monthly Vinyl Releases is an independent project and is not affiliated with any of the shops listed. Prices, stock and release dates come from the shops' public new-release pages and are updated every morning. They may have changed since; the shop's own page is binding.

No account, no cookies. Your bag and settings are stored only in your browser. In your bag you can save a backup link: the data is inside the link, nothing is stored on a server.

Covers and audio previews load directly from the shops' servers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

To improve the page, we count visits and clicks anonymously with Umami: no cookies, no IP addresses stored, nothing that identifies you, and no data sold or shared.
